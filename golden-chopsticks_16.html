Our Story

A culinary journey through ancient China

Born from a deep reverence for Chinese culinary tradition, Golden Chopsticks was founded on a singular belief — that food is the most intimate form of storytelling.

Every dish we craft is a love letter to the provinces of China, drawing from the bold heat of Sichuan, the delicate sweetness of Cantonese dim sum, and the smoky umami of Shanghainese cuisine.

The Artisan

Crafted by Gopal Pradhan

At the heart of Golden Chopsticks is Chef Gopal Pradhan — a culinary visionary whose hands have shaped every recipe on our menu. With a deep reverence for authentic Asian flavours and a flair for bold, modern presentation, Chef Gopal brings each dish to life with passion and precision.

Born and raised in Siliguri, his cooking is a love letter to the flavours of the East — from the smoky woks of Hakka kitchens to the delicate folds of a perfect shumai. Every plate that leaves his kitchen carries a story.
